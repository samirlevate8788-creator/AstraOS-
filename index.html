THE WORKSTATION

Familiar Linux.
AstraOS character.

A focused XFCE desktop with AstraOS identity, familiar window and workspace controls, office apps, a guided toolkit, and a practice site served only to your own computer.

01 / DESKTOP
▰

Linux at the foundation

Debian 13 and XFCE provide the system base. AstraOS adds a polished desktop workflow and familiar shortcuts while keeping Linux tools, packages and files accessible.

02 / PRACTICE
⌁

Loopback lab target

Start a small sample website bound to 127.0.0.1. Nmap, Gobuster, and packet-capture examples stay pointed at this local practice target.
