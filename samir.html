Samir Levate

Founder & Project Lead, AstraOS

Samir Levate is a Computer Science and Engineering student building AstraOS, an educational open-source Linux desktop project. He leads the project's direction, release planning, documentation and community work, with a focus on helping learners explore Linux and practical operating-system workflows.

AstraOS 0.1.1 is a bootable Debian 13 live desktop with an AstraOS-branded XFCE session. Its early releases focus on a working live system and a clear foundation for future utilities and developer tools.
