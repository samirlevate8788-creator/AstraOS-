Privacy Policy

A plain-language summary of this static project website.

What this site collects

The AstraOS website is static and has no account system, payment flow, mailing list, analytics integration, or first-party cookies. It does not save a consent choice in browser storage. GitHub Pages may process technical request data such as IP address and user agent to deliver and protect the site under GitHub's own policies.

Cookies and analytics

The project site does not run analytics or set its own tracking cookies. Your browser may still store data for services you choose to open, and the hosting provider processes requests under its own policies.

External services

Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs. Opening an external link sends a browser request to that service; those providers apply their own privacy policies.

Contact

The Support page opens a prefilled email draft in your mail app. Nothing is submitted to AstraOS by the static website; you choose whether to send the draft. Do not include passwords, private keys, or other sensitive information.

External links

Review an external provider's policy before sharing information. For AstraOS privacy questions, use the AstraOS support page.

This notice describes the current project website, not Debian, GitHub, Google, or other providers.
