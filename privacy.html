Privacy Policy

A plain-language summary of this static project website.

What this site collects

The AstraOS website is a static GitHub Pages site. It has no account system, payment flow, mailing list, or first-party analytics currently configured. GitHub Pages may process technical request data such as IP address and user agent to deliver and protect the site under GitHub's own policies.

Optional analytics

Optional Google Analytics is disabled unless the site maintainer configures a GA4 measurement ID and you choose “Accept analytics.” Your choice is kept in this browser's local storage. Before consent, the analytics script is not loaded. You can change your choice with “Privacy choices” in the footer. If analytics is enabled, Google may set cookies and process usage data under its own terms.

External services

Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs. Opening GitHub, LinkedIn, or another external link shares your browser request with that service. Those providers apply their own privacy policies.

Contact

The contact form validates in your browser and opens a prefilled email draft. Nothing is submitted to AstraOS by that form; you choose whether to send the draft using your email provider. Do not include passwords, private keys, or other sensitive information.

Your choices

Use the footer's Privacy choices control to accept or reject optional analytics. Clearing this site's local storage also clears the saved choice. You may contact the project through its GitHub issue tracker for privacy questions.

This notice describes the current project website, not Debian, GitHub, Google, or other providers.
