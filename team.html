Meet The AstraOS Team

Meet the passionate developers building AstraOS together.

Our Mission

We are building AstraOS as an educational open-source operating system where students and developers can learn, collaborate and innovate together.

Leadership Team

The people leading the AstraOS project.

Samir Levate

👑 Founder & Project Lead

Samir Levate is the Founder and Project Lead of AstraOS, an educational open-source operating system project. He leads the project's vision, system architecture, Titan Kernel planning, documentation, roadmap, community management and technical development. His goal is to build a modern operating system while creating a collaborative platform where students and developers can learn low-level programming, operating system concepts and real-world open-source development together.

Samarth Waghmode

🤝 Co-Founder

Samarth Waghmode serves as the Co-Founder of AstraOS and works closely with the Founder on project planning, technical discussions, research, contributor support and long-term development strategy. He plays an important role in strengthening collaboration, improving project organization and helping AstraOS grow into a modern educational open-source operating system for students and developers.
