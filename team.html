Samir Levate

Founder & Project Lead, AstraOS

Samir is a Computer Science and Engineering student building AstraOS, an open-source Debian desktop project for everyday office work, development and responsible security learning.

The project is being developed in public, with a practical focus on an accessible desktop, a local training lab and tools that teach defensive skills. The newest work is still a source build; it needs a fresh ISO build and validation before it is ready to download.
